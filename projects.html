Client work, still under wraps

Most of my full-time years went into products that never make it into a portfolio — from retail counters and restaurant floors to the back-office systems that run a whole company. Here’s the ground it covers and the parts I worked on.

Retail & point of sale

NDA

The software that runs a retail counter — ringing up sales, the display the customer watches while it happens, and the web store behind it. Most of the work sat in the edge cases: split tender, returns, staying usable when the network drops.

  • POS terminal
  • Customer display
  • Web store

Quick-service restaurants

NDA

Order screens for fast-food counters, and the kitchen display that catches those orders. Big touch targets, no scrolling, readable from a step back.

  • Self-order kiosk
  • Kitchen display (KDS)

ERP & operations

NDA

The core operations platform — procurement, invoicing, reporting, all wired into one another. Years of feature requests to keep coherent instead of a pile of switches.

  • ERP platform
  • Ops dashboards

Sales & CRM

NDA

Where a sales team lives: leads, pipeline stages, follow-ups, quotes. The job is making a hundred small updates a day feel like two clicks each.

  • CRM
  • Sales pipeline
  • Quotes

HR & payroll (HRMS)

NDA

Attendance, leave, payroll and the org chart. One calm face for the employee checking a payslip, a denser one for HR running month-end.

  • Attendance
  • Payroll
  • Leave & approvals

Inventory & warehousing

NDA

Stock counts, SKUs, purchase orders, transfers between locations. The numbers here have to be exactly right or everything downstream breaks.

  • Stock & SKUs
  • Purchase orders
  • Stock takes

Logistics & delivery

NDA

A dispatch board for the office, a stripped-down app for the driver, and live tracking for whoever’s waiting on the order.

  • Dispatch board
  • Driver app
